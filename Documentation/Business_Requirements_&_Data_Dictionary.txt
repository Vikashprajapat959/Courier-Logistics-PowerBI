## 1. PROJECT OBJECTIVE & PROBLEM STATEMENT

The objective of this project is to develop an end-to-end Executive Supply Chain Analytics Platform for analyzing and optimizing supply chain and logistics operations.

The platform focuses on identifying delivery bottlenecks, monitoring shipping costs, analyzing product damage and returns, and evaluating warehouse inventory health to support better operational decision-making.

The dashboard helps management answer critical business questions across three major analytical areas:

**Page 1: Executive Financials & Shipping Cost Trends**
- Revenue and net profit performance.
- Shipping cost analysis.
- Profit margin monitoring.
- Financial performance across categories and regions.

**Page 2: Logistics Carrier SLAs, Delays & Root-Cause Analysis**
- On-time delivery performance.
- Logistics carrier performance.
- Delivery delay analysis.
- Identification of major delay reasons.
- Product damage and return monitoring.

**Page 3: Warehouse Capacity, Inventory Risks & Supplier Reliability**
- Warehouse inventory monitoring.
- Inventory versus safety stock analysis.
- Identification of stock-out risks.
- Supplier reliability evaluation.
- Inventory health and operational risk assessment.

The ultimate goal is to transform raw supply chain data into meaningful business insights through interactive Power BI dashboards.

## 2. KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS (KPIs) & BUSINESS LOGIC

The dashboard uses DAX measures to calculate important business performance indicators.

### 1. On-Time Delivery (OTD %)

**Formula:**

`OTD % = (Total On-Time Orders / Total Orders) × 100`

**Target Benchmark:** 85% or higher.

Purpose:
Measures the percentage of orders delivered within their expected delivery timelines.

### 2. Net Profit

**Formula:**

`Net Profit = Revenue - (Item Cost + Shipping Cost)`

Purpose:
Measures the actual profit generated after accounting for item and shipping costs.

### 3. Net Profit Margin %

**Formula:**

`Net Profit Margin % = (Total Net Profit / Total Item Revenue) × 100`

Purpose:
Evaluates how efficiently the business converts revenue into profit.

### 4. Product Damage Rate %

**Formula:**

`Product Damage Rate % = (Count of Damaged Items / Total Orders Received) × 100`

Purpose:
Monitors product damage during transportation and delivery.

### 5. Order Return Rate %

**Formula:**

`Order Return Rate % = (Count of Returned Items / Total Orders Received) × 100`

Purpose:
Measures the percentage of received orders that are returned.

**Note:** KPI calculations must use the correct dataset grain. If the dataset contains multiple items per order, item-level and order-level measures should be calculated separately.

## 3. DATA DICTIONARY (CRITICAL METRICS EXPLAINED)

The dataset contains operational and transactional attributes used for supply chain performance analysis.

The following fields describe important business metrics:

| Attribute | Description |
|---|---|
| OrderID | Unique identifier assigned to each customer order. |
| IsDelayed (Yes/No) | Identifies whether actual delivery exceeded the expected delivery time. |
| DelayReason | Records the primary reason for delivery delays, such as severe weather, customs delay, or traffic. |
| IsDamaged (Yes/No) | Indicates whether a product was damaged during transportation. |
| ReturnStatus (Returned/None) | Tracks product return information for reverse logistics analysis. |
| WarehouseInventoryLevel | Represents the available inventory units at a warehouse. |
| SafetyStockLevel | Minimum inventory threshold required to reduce the risk of stock-outs. |
| SupplierReliabilityScore | Represents the supplier's reliability score, measured on a scale from 0 to 1. |

### Important Business Logic

- **Delivery Delay:** An order is considered delayed when its actual delivery time exceeds its expected delivery time.
- **Inventory Risk:** If warehouse inventory falls below the safety stock level, the warehouse is flagged for inventory risk.
- **Supplier Reliability:** Supplier reliability scores help compare supplier performance.
- **Product Damage:** Damage records help identify transportation and handling quality issues.
- **Product Returns:** Return records help monitor reverse logistics performance.

## 4. ARCHITECTURE & TARGET AUDIENCE

### Data Model Architecture

The project follows a Star Schema data modeling approach to organize supply chain information and support efficient reporting.

**Data Model Structure:**
- One Fact Table containing transactional information.
- Dimension Tables containing descriptive business information.
- Relationships connecting transactional records with relevant dimensions.

### Data Scale

- Transactional order records from the supply chain dataset.
- Multiple operational attributes.
- Financial, delivery, inventory, and supplier-related information.

### Target Audience

The dashboard is designed for business professionals and decision-makers, including:

- Chief Operations Officer (COO).
- Logistics Directors.
- Supply Chain Managers.
- Regional Warehouse Managers.
- Inventory Planning Teams.
- Business Intelligence Analysts.

### Business Value

This project demonstrates how Power BI can transform operational data into actionable insights by combining data modeling, DAX calculations, interactive visualizations, and business performance monitoring.
